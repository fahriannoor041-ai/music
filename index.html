"this gift is my way of showing how grateful i am.

to remind you how worthy you are of love,
to show that not everyone walks away,
and to tell you that i choose to stay, even at your worst.

still, this gift can never fully express how precious you are to me,
nor how deeply i adore you.

you are the love of my life, and no one could ever replace you.

some say they would die for you, others say they would kill for you.
for you, i choose to live in this cruel world.
i choose kindness, i choose growth, i choose to be better.

you mean the world to me.
you complete me.
and most importantly, you love me without hesitation —
and i love you more than anything, tiara hanifah."

🎞️ Lihat Momen Kita
